torchvic.crop is a PyTorch port of the WOFOST 7.2 crop model as implemented in
PCSE (Python Crop Simulation Environment) version 6.0.13.

  PCSE: Copyright (c) 2004-2024 Wageningen Environmental Research,
  Wageningen-UR. Authors: Allard de Wit and Herman Berghuijs.
  Licence: European Union Public Licence v1.2 (EUPL-1.2).
  Source: https://github.com/ajwdewit/pcse

The EUPL-1.2 appendix lists GPL-3.0 as a compatible licence, which allows this
derivative work to be distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later (the licence of
TorchVIC) provided the PCSE copyright and this credit are kept. The modules
ported are pcse/crop/{wofost72,phenology,partitioning,assimilation,
respiration,evapotranspiration,leaf_dynamics,stem_dynamics,root_dynamics,
storage_organ_dynamics}.py, pcse/soil/classic_waterbalance.py (validation
mode only) and astro/AFGEN from pcse/util.py.

Crop parameter sets (data, not code) are read from the WOFOST crop parameter
repository, https://github.com/ajwdewit/WOFOST_crop_parameters, branch
wofost72 (EUPL, Wageningen Environmental Research); a copy sits in
<data root>/static/upstream/wofost_crop_parameters/wofost72.
